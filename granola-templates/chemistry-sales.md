Chemistry-Strategy or Sales Call — Granola template

For chemistry, strategy calls and sales conversations, both individual (1:1 coaching
prospects) and organizational (workshop/leadership program prospects).

## Meeting Context (paste as the template's context paragraph)

A chemistry, strategy or sales conversation between Dawn Garibaldi (Amplify Strategy
Group) and a prospective client — either an individual exploring 1:1
executive coaching, or an organizational contact exploring a workshop or
leadership development engagement. Use only what is actually in the notes;
do not invent anything.

## Sections (in order — use `###` heading syntax, not bold)

### Headline
One line: Date (year-month-day format), who this prospect is, what they're exploring, and where it stands.

### Prospect & Context
Name, Date (year-month-day format), company/role if organizational, how they found Dawn or who referred them, what type of engagement is being discussed (1:1 coaching, workshop,
leadership program).

### What They're Trying to Solve
The problem or goal in their own framing, not Dawn's interpretation of it.

### Budget & Timeline Signals
Anything said about budget range, decision timeline, or decision-makers
involved. If nothing was said, say so — do not estimate or guess.

### Objections or Hesitations
Anything raised as a concern or reason to hesitate.

### Key Quotes
Verbatim lines that reveal how the prospect is thinking about the decision.

### Fit Assessment
Dawn's honest read on whether this is a good-fit prospect (premium fee
willingness, boutique/high-touch fit) — brief, not a formal scorecard.

### My Follow-ups
What Dawn committed to send or do next (proposal, follow-up call, references).
Kept separate from the prospect's own next steps below.

### Their Next Steps
What the prospect said they'd do next (check with a partner, review budget,
get back to Dawn by a date).

### Personal Notes
Anything worth remembering about them as a person, independent of the deal.

### Reflection
Dawn's own honest, brief read on how the call went and what it suggests
about the close likelihood. If nothing stands out, say so.
