# Client Session — Granola template

For 1:1 coaching sessions, including 1099-partner-referred (for example - TGP, Positive
Intelligence) client calls.

## Meeting Context (paste as the template's context paragraph)

A 1:1 executive coaching session between Dawn Garibaldi and a coaching client.
May be a Dawn-direct client or a client referred through a 1099 partner
engagement (Talent Growth Partners, Positive Intelligence, or another partner).
Use only what is actually in the notes; do not invent anything.

## Sections (in order — use `###` heading syntax, not bold)

### Headline
One line: who the client is, what this session covered, and the upshot. This
is the only section Dawn expects to reread six weeks from now.

### Client & Session
Client name, date (year-month-day), engagement/partner (if 1099-referred, which one), session number or stage if mentioned. 

### Follow-up on Prior Commitments
What was open from the last session, and what happened with it. If nothing
was open or nothing was covered, say so — do not invent continuity.

### Summary
Plain-language recap of what was discussed, 3-5 sentences.

### What's Really Going On
The underlying issue beneath the stated topic, if the notes surface one. If
nothing beneath the surface came up, say so.

### Upcoming Events for Client
Is there an upcoming event that's important for the client: this could be a presentation, special meeting, vacation, business trip. If it's mentioned in the call I would like it highlighted here. If not mentioned, say so.

### What Shifted
Any visible change in the client's thinking, framing, or stance during the
session. If nothing shifted, say so plainly — do not manufacture progress.

### Progress Toward Their Goal
Concrete movement (or lack of it) against the client's stated coaching goal.

### Key Quotes
Verbatim lines worth remembering, in the client's own words.

### Possible Testimonials
Anything the client said about Dawn's coaching or Amplify Strategy Group,
captured in their exact words. If nothing like this came up, say so — do not
paraphrase into something that sounds like a testimonial.

### Frameworks & Tools Used
Any named coaching model, assessment, or tool referenced or applied (e.g.
SDI/Core Strengths, Hogan, a specific framework).

### Client Action Items
What the client committed to before the next session. Separate from Dawn's
own follow-ups below.

### My Follow-ups
What Dawn committed to — anything to send, prepare, or check on before the
next session. Kept separate from the client's list on purpose: a merged list
is where commitments get lost.

### Personal Notes
Life details worth remembering for the relationship — not clinical, just
what a person who cares would remember.

### Next Session Prep
Anything to have ready or reread before the next session.

### Reflection
Dawn's own honest, brief read on how the session went. If there's nothing
notable to reflect on, say so.

## Confidentiality note
Client-derived content in this template (Key Quotes, Possible Testimonials,
Personal Notes) is confidential by default. Anything pulled from here into
the story bank or a testimonial file must be anonymized/theme-level unless
Dawn has explicit permission from the client to use their name or specifics.
